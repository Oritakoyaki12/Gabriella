myths vs. facts.

The truth is worth sharing. Start with these three.

myth

You can tell who has HIV by looking.

fact

A test is the only way to know.

myth

HIV spreads through hugs or sharing food.

fact

HIV isn't spread through everyday contact.

myth

An HIV diagnosis means your life is over.

fact

With treatment, people with HIV can live long, healthy lives.
